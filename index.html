🔍 What is fanwan?

fanwan is a delightful open-source web application that lets you create your own virtual "rice bowl" for collecting donations. Whether you're a student facing tough times, a creative soul seeking support, or just someone wanting to try something new, fanwan makes the process fun and community-driven. The name comes from a lively spirit: "没得饭吃啷个办？先把饭碗儿摆出来嘛" - which roughly translates to "No food to eat? Well, put your bowl out there!" This captures the heart of the app: putting your needs out there in a positive, approachable way.

❓ Troubleshooting & FAQs

🔒 Why won't it open?

If the page doesn't load, double-check your internet connection. Also, make sure your web browser is updated. Sometimes, a pop-up blocker might prevent the app from opening - turn it off temporarily and try again.

⚠️ I see an error message - what should I do?

Don't panic! Error messages are common. Screenshot them and go to the "Issues" section on the GitHub page (the same page you downloaded from). You can report the issue, and the community will help you fix it. Or, simply close everything and reopen the app.

🌍 Does it work on other devices?

Absolutely! fanwan is a web app, so as long as you have a browser, it will work on Windows, Mac, Linux, tablets, and phones. The experience is optimized for desktop, but it works everywhere.
